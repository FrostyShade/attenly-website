Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Attenly for Android

1. On-Device First Architecture

Attenly is designed so students can track attendance, weekly timetables, coursework deadlines, Course Map progress, Study Mode logs, and Vault study materials without creating an account. Your personal academic records stay stored locally on your Android device unless you explicitly export or share them.

2. Timetable Exports & Sharing

When you manually export a schedule (for example, attenly_timetable.json), only class names, timings, days, and room labels are included. Personal attendance history, absence counts, assignments, and Vault files are never bundled into timetable exports.

3. Optional Group Projects & Google Sign-In

Group Projects requires Google sign-in and an internet connection strictly to create, join via invite code, and sync shared project workspaces (such as To do / In progress / Done boards, blockers, and shared folders) across teammates. Teammates inside a project can view only content added directly to that shared Group Project. Personal records outside Group Projects remain local to your device.

4. Google Play Purchases & Ads

Optional upgrades such as Attenly Pro (including Leave Planner simulations and the Pro app icon) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store payment card numbers. Details for authorized advertising partners are published in app-ads.txt.

5. Data Sale & Third Parties

We never sell personal information or academic records to data brokers or unrelated third parties. Third-party infrastructure (such as Google Play Services and cloud authentication/sync when using Group Projects) operates only to deliver the core functionality you trigger.

6. Website Cookies

This landing website at getattenly.in is static and operates without advertising trackers or invasive behavioral cookies. Outbound clicks to Google Play or social profiles follow each destination platform's own privacy notice.

7. Your Choices, Deletion & Contact

  • Uninstalling the app clears local on-device records unless backed up on your device.
  • To request deletion of shared Group Project account data or ask any privacy question, contact developer Paul Baruah at attenly.support@gmail.com.
