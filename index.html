Quiet solo focus, clean team sync

Two halves of the semester: Botanical Study Mode when locking in alone, and clean coordination with classmates.

Dedicated Study Mode

  • Custom focus blocks: build a weekly study plan around your subjects with 15, 25, 45, or 60 minute timers.
  • Planned breaks & reminders: schedule breaks directly between sessions and get timely nudges to stay on track.
  • Full-screen landscape timer: flip your phone sideways for a distraction-free desk countdown clock and review session logs later.

Coursework, Vault & Team Projects

  • Course Map & exam prep: break your syllabus into units and topics, mark exam scope, and track what you have revised.
  • Vault study library: keep PDFs, slides, images, links, and notes organized by folder, saved right from other apps.
  • Group Projects (free): join teams via invite code, track To do, In progress, and Done, flag blockers, link shared folders, and edit offline with auto-sync.
  • Privacy-safe sharing & Leave Planner Pro: export class schedules as JSON without attendance history, and test future leave scenarios with Pro.

Frequently asked questions

No account is required for attendance tracking, weekly timetables, assignments, Study Mode, Course Map, or Vault. Personal records remain stored on your device. Only Group Projects uses Google sign-in and an internet connection when creating, joining, or syncing shared team boards (and saved project work can still be edited offline).

Yes. Go to Profile → Subjects, select the subject you want to update, and open its Calendar view to log or adjust attendance for earlier dates.

Each subject automatically tracks your percentage against your custom target (default 75%) and tells you how many classes you must attend or can safely skip. Attenly Pro unlocks Leave Planner to estimate how upcoming planned absences could affect your percentage. Predictions are estimates; always verify with your institution's official portal.

Attendance tracking, timetables, assignments, Study Mode & Pomodoro timer, Course Map, Vault, and Group Projects are all available without Pro. Attenly Pro unlocks Leave Planner simulations and the custom Pro app icon.

Exported attenly_timetable.json files contain only class subjects, timings, rooms, and days—never your attendance history, assignments, or Vault files. Inside Group Projects, teammates only see content added directly to that shared project.

Attenly supports Android 7.0 and above and is built to stay fast and lightweight on everyday devices.
