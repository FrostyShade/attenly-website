FAQ

Questions? We got you.

Everything students ask before downloading.

No account, no login, no internet required. Attenly is fully offline and stores everything locally on your device. Your data belongs to you.
You set your minimum attendance threshold (typically 75%). Attenly tracks your current attendance per subject and calculates how many classes you can still miss without falling below that threshold. It updates live every time you mark a class.
Attenly has a free version with all the core attendance, timetable, and assignment features you need. A Pro version is also available with additional features and a different app icon.
Completely. Attenly does not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers. Your attendance records, timetable, and assignments never leave your phone unless you manually export them using the Share feature.
Yes! You can export your timetable as a JSON file and share it with classmates. Only class names, rooms, timings, and days are exported — your attendance data and assignments are never included in the shared file.
You can set your own threshold per subject. Most colleges require 75%, which is the default, but you can customise it per subject if different courses have different requirements.
Attenly is available on Google Play and works on Android devices. It's designed to be lightweight so it runs smoothly even on older or budget Android phones.
